AI Image Bias: Clock & Wine Glass

The 10:10 Clock Phenomenon

When you ask AI image generators to create a clock, they almost always show 10:10. This isn't random.

  • Historical Context: Since 1926 (Hamilton) through 1950s (Rolex), watch companies used this angle in advertisements
  • Dataset Bias: AI trained on images where 10:10 is most common
  • Edinburgh University Study: "Most AI models fail to represent clock times accurately or stick to specific patterns"

[Clock Examples Showing 10:10 Bias]

Image: clock-bias-examples.jpg

The Full Wine Glass Problem

Another challenging task: drawing a wine glass filled to the brim.

  • Limited Training Data: Most images show partially filled glasses
  • Physics Complexity: Glass reflection, transparency, liquid refraction
  • Technical Issue: AI draws the glass shape first, then tries to fill it - causing boundary calculation errors

[Wine Glass Rendering Failures]

Image: wine-glass-examples.jpg

Nano Banana 2 Breakthrough

Recently, the Nano Banana 2 model successfully generated both correct clock times and full wine glasses - showing significant progress in physical consistency understanding.
